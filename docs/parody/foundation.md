# The parody universe: the first five (16 October 2026)

Ryan's brief: a fictional parody wrestling universe, legally distinct from real promotions, people and trademarks, built on satire and exaggeration. The "Parody target" line is for the design team only. It never goes into the game's data or text.

Rules kept for every entry: no real name, no exact sound-alike, no copyrighted or trademarked nickname, catchphrase or move name; the joke comments on the original.

---

## 1. Brother Bronzer

1. **Parody target:** the 1980s blond, bandana-wearing hero of the big-muscle era.
2. **In-game name:** Brother Bronzer.
3. **Gimmick category:** Larger-than-life hero (Babyface).
4. **Gimmick description:** A deeply tanned, permanently flexing icon who tells children to say their prayers, eat their vitamins and buy his frying pan. He has headlined for thirty years and has never once been seen losing clean. Backstage, his contract has a clause for everything, including the lighting.
5. **Catchphrase:** "What'cha gonna do, little brother, when the bronze rubs off on YOU?"
6. **Finisher name:** The Thirty-Year Leg Drop.
7. **Finisher description:** He cups a hand to his ear for a full minute and rips his shirt in two. Then he drops a slow, flat leg across the throat of a man who has already been pinned in his mind.

## 2. Tepid Tex Granite

1. **Parody target:** the 1990s beer-drinking, boss-hating anti-hero.
2. **In-game name:** Tepid Tex Granite.
3. **Gimmick category:** Blue-collar rebel (Babyface, plays dirty).
4. **Gimmick description:** A bald, foul-tempered roofer from a town nobody can find on a map. He hates authority, paperwork and anyone in a suit. He drinks room-temperature soda by the crate because the cooler is always broken, and he takes it out on management every Monday.
5. **Catchphrase:** "That's the bottom of the cooler, and it's WARM!"
6. **Finisher name:** The Stunned Silence.
7. **Finisher description:** A kick to the gut, a quarter turn, and he drops to the seat, jolting his opponent's jaw off his shoulder. He then empties two cans of flat soda over the body.

## 3. Kale Kenway, the Organic Underdog

1. **Parody target:** the bearded, plant-eating, crowd-chanting indie hero who was told he was too small.
2. **In-game name:** Kale Kenway.
3. **Gimmick category:** Indie darling (Babyface).
4. **Gimmick description:** A small, bearded technician who grows his own vegetables and lives in a van to save money. He has been told he is "not a top guy" by every office he has worked for, so the crowd has made him one. He wrestles every match as if it is his last, and his knees agree.
5. **Catchphrase:** He points to the sky and the arena chants "YAY! YAY! YAY!", and he cannot hear himself think.
6. **Finisher name:** The Compost Lock.
7. **Finisher description:** He traps one arm with his legs, cranks the face back with a forearm, and asks politely whether they would like to tap before he composts them.

## 4. Chairman Moneybags McStrut

1. **Parody target:** the billionaire owner who books himself on his own show.
2. **In-game name:** Chairman Moneybags McStrut.
3. **Gimmick category:** Corrupt executive (Heel).
4. **Gimmick description:** The founder, owner and only shareholder of everything, who "does not care about wrestling" and spends every show on screen. He fires people on live television, rehires them after the commercial and makes them thank him. He has won a world title, and nobody remembers how.
5. **Catchphrase:** "You're... FIRED. Effective after the merchandise sale."
6. **Finisher name:** The Power Strut.
7. **Finisher description:** He swaggers to the ring with his arms swinging like a broken windmill while three men in suits do the actual beating. Then he strikes a pose over the body and takes the credit.

## 5. Mr. Lastrites, the Funeral Director

1. **Parody target:** the dark, undying, supernatural legend with the long entrance.
2. **In-game name:** Mr. Lastrites.
3. **Gimmick category:** Spooky phenom (Heel).
4. **Gimmick description:** A towering funeral director for hire who has "died" eleven times and comes back every spring with a longer entrance. The lights go out, the organ plays and the commercial break runs long. He is all business, and the business is funeral packages with optional extras.
5. **Catchphrase:** "Rest... in... instalments."
6. **Finisher name:** The Final Invoice.
7. **Finisher description:** He turns his victim upside down, sits them into a piledriver with his arms crossed over their chest, rolls his own eyes back into his head, and leaves a bill on the body for the casket.

---

**Balance:** three Babyfaces (Bronzer, Granite, Kenway) and two Heels (McStrut, Lastrites), across five eras and styles.
