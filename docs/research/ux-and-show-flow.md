# UX and show flow: research notes

## Sources (read 13 October 2026)
- OSFTW review of Total Extreme Wrestling 2008: https://www.osftw.com/reviews/432/total-extreme-wrestling-2008. It says some tasks take two or three screens when one would do. It says mouse clicking improved with each version but "is not quite on the level of greatness yet".
- Bonus Stage review of TEW 2016: https://www.bonusstage.co.uk/archives/68332. It calls the interface "basic and unsightly for a game dominated by text". It praises how flexible the game is.

## What we take from them
These are our reading of the reviews, not tested facts.
- The genre leader's known weak spot is clicks per task and a dated text interface. A show that runs itself, with a styled broadcast and stops only at decisions, is a clear point of difference.
- Fewer clicks must not cost decisions. Auto-play never answers a call (`app/tests/auto.js` checks it).
