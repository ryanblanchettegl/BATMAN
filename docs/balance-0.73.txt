What changed (models and finance code only):
1. The mutiny meeting: when the owner calms the room, the room is properly settled (mood up, stress down, the clock cleared).
   Before, the clock was never reset, so the meeting came back every few weeks and cost 6 owner trust each time
   (84 to 90 trust over 100 weeks for the underdog; this alone got it fired).
2. How the owner reads a show (src/65-you.js): a middling show (a C, verdict band -1) costs a little trust (0.25 a show
   before weighting); a dud or worse costs less steeply than before (0.35 a point, at most 2).
3. Overheads that grow with income are 4% lower (src/40-week.js), so a company on suggested cards keeps a thin margin.
