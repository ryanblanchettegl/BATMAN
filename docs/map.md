# Map of the engine

One line per file in `src/`, in build order: what it owns, then the `E.` functions it adds. Made by `node tools/map.js`; run it again after adding a file.

- **00-core.js**: How good a match or a segment was is shown to the player as stars, in quarter steps, never as a percentage. `E.newGame`
- **10-match.js**: extension hooks: later systems plug into the match engine here
- **20-story.js**: angles: the story director
- **21-living.js**: living storylines (docs/plans/living-storylines.md) A feud has no length and no planned week. `E.feudBook`, `E.feudPlan`, `E.feudPhase`, `E.feudEmber`, `E.startStory`, `E.heatStory`, `E.storyPlanEnd`, `E.setMatchEnd`, `E.matchFeud`, `E.storyPeople`, `E.storyPreview`, `E.storyStart`, `E.season`, `E.storyEnd`, `E.tonight`, `E.storyActs`, `E.storyDo`, `E.stipFits`
- **30-show.js**: running a show
- **31-live.js**: The live show The player's show is not worked out in one go. `E.liveBegin`, `E.liveNext`, `E.liveDecide`, `E.liveInfo`
- **32-direction.js**: The direction of the company Two calls from the gorilla position that decide more than tonight (src/31-live.js runs them): The owner on the headset. `E.faceInfo`
- **33-ring.js**: Calls in the ring Three more calls from the gorilla position (src/31-live.js runs them), each caused by what is true of the match: Somebody is hurt.
- **34-night.js**: wraps: 31-live.js (it must sort after them; test-buildorder.js checks) `E.liveBegin`, `E.afterShow`, `E.comingUp`, `E.nightSeen`
- **40-week.js**: the week `E.endWeek`, `E.resolveEvent`
- **42-events.js**: Big events belong to a company (NEXT.md, job 3) Each promotion has its own twelve monthly big events in P.events: a name, a type and an optional rule. `E.events`, `E.evSet`, `E.EV_TYPES`, `E.buyLog`, `E.evSpend`, `E.evHype`
- **50-company.js**: the company: broadcast slot, production, risk, tickets, advertising, sponsors `E.setCompany`, `E.sponsorOk`, `E.acceptSponsor`, `E.dropSponsor`, `E.slotOdds`, `E.askSlot`, `E.company`
- **55-preshow.js**: before the bell: something goes wrong (or right) and you make a call `E.preShow`, `E.resolvePre`
- **60-grammar.js**: The text grammar: procedurally written lines for the newsletter, the websites and the chat room A small grammar engine in the manner of Tracery, with Improv's idea that a line may only be used when it is true of the...
- **60-rpg.js**: attempts: a chance of success built from what helps and what hurts, shown before you choose Under the hood it is two six-sided numbers plus modifiers against a target; the player only ever sees the chance. `E.repackOdds`, `E.repackage`, `E.setManager`, `E.mouthpieces`, `E.gimFit`, `E.GIMS`, `E.TRAITS`, `E.stableOf`, `E.micOf`, `E.odds`
- **65-you.js**: you: the booker, the owner you answer to, and booking power `E.spendPoint`, `E.matchOdds`, `E.cardCost`, `E.setCreed`, `E.budget`, `E.lobbyOdds`, `E.lobby`, `E.jobOffers`, `E.advice`, `E.STYLES`, `E.ROOTS`, `E.PLEDGE`, `E.SKILLS`, `E.xpNeed`, `E.bpGrant`, `E.questNow`
- **66-relations.js**: The relationship matrix (S.rm) and what people remember How two people feel about each other is the centre of the game. `E.bond`, `E.bondWord`, `E.rel`, `E.relations`, `E.relBump`, `E.youRemember`
- **70-season.js**: contender rankings `E.rankFor`, `E.tournOk`, `E.startTourn`, `E.powerYears`, `E.power`, `E.powerWas`, `E.campInfo`, `E.sendCamp`, `E.callUp`, `E.FOCUS`, `E.tournActive`
- **75-people.js**: people: ageing, retirement, each year's rookie class, and what your scouts can tell you `E.intel`, `E.scoutInfo`, `E.scout`, `E.HIDDEN`, `E.phaseOf`, `E.PHASE`
- **76-friends.js**: friends on a collision course A friend of the champion is now the top challenger for their title.
- **76-locker.js**: the locker room: roles, the ego grid, stress, bodies that wear out, and the people who patch them up `E.ego`, `E.lockerRoom`, `E.ROLE`, `E.ZONES`, `E.INTN`, `E.MEDN`, `E.zones`, `E.medInfo`, `E.rest`, `E.workHurtOk`, `E.workHurt`, `E.mentorsFor`, `E.setMentor`, `E.wordOdds`, `E.haveWord`, `E.family`
- **76-student.js**: the student passes the teacher When somebody a wrestler trained becomes more over than they are, it reaches the booker's desk once: have the teacher put the student over, leave it alone (the teacher notices, and it...
