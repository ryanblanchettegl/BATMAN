# Proposal: trouble in public (wish 77)

**Status:** for Ryan to decide, mainly on tone. Not built.

## The idea

Now and then a wrestler makes the wrong kind of news. The player chooses what the company does: suspend them, bury them in the card, or stand by them. Results last for months.

## Keeping it light

The game is a booking sim in a retro style. These stories should read like old-time scandal sheets, not like real harm. Ideas that fit:

- A wrestler is caught in a loud argument with a venue manager.
- A wrestler says something rude about the fans on a radio show.
- A wrestler is photographed somewhere they said they would not be.
- A wrestler fights with a rival at a restaurant.

Nothing about crimes, drugs, violence against people, politics or real-world groups. Names, towns and details are invented.

## How it would work

An inbox event with three choices:

1. **Suspend** for two to four weeks. Morale of the room goes up a little, the wrestler's goes down, the story fades.
2. **Bury** them for a month. They stay on the shows in a weak spot.
3. **Stand by them.** An attempt with a chance. If it works, the fans take the wrestler's side and merchandise rises. If it fails, the company's popularity takes a small hit.

## Questions for Ryan

1. Is this tone right, or should there be no scandal stories at all?
2. Should a wrestler's earlier choices (a grudge, a loyalty promise) change the odds?
