# Proposal: owner mode (wish 93)

**Status:** for Ryan to decide. Not built.

## The idea

When the company becomes yours, the job changes. You hire a booker for a second brand, set their goals, and sell or buy shares. The game gains a second layer: you are no longer building every card, you are choosing who does.

## What exists today

- The takeover already happens in a long game. `S.owner.me` switches on and the owner's trust meter goes away.
- The assistant booker in wish 94 and fast mode in `TASKS.md` task 3 are the pieces that let a booker run a brand without you.

## What owner mode would add

- A second brand with its own roster, shows and card.
- A hired booker with skills, a style and a temper. You give them goals for the quarter and they report back.
- Shares: sell a slice to raise cash, buy it back later. Investors have opinions (wish 53).
- A choice each week of which brand you book yourself.

## Why this needs a decision

It is the largest wish on the list. It depends on task 3 (the new booking screen and fast mode), wish 53 (investors) and wish 94 (the assistant). Building it before those would mean building it twice.

## Questions for Ryan

1. Is owner mode the end game, or an option from the start?
2. One extra brand, or as many as you can afford?
3. Should it wait until task 3 is done?
