# Proposal: four kinds of fan (wish 69)

**Status:** for Ryan to decide. Not built.

## The idea

Today the crowd is one number. Split it into four groups: casuals, diehards, families and kids. Each group likes a different show, and each company model weights them differently. The Company overview shows four bars, so the player can see who is in the building and who is leaving.

## What it would change

- A match is scored four times, once for each group, then blended by the company's mix. A technical classic scores high with diehards and low with kids. A brawl with a big finish scores high with casuals.
- Each model has a mix. The corporate giant leans on families and casuals. The diehards' company leans on diehards. Lucha spectacle leans on kids and casuals. Outlaw leans on diehards and casuals.
- A group that is ignored for a month drifts away. A group that is courted comes back slowly.
- Gate, merchandise and television would each read the groups differently. Families buy merchandise. Diehards buy tickets. Casuals watch television.

## Why this needs a decision

It changes the crowd maths in every system: match quality, expectations, `calibrate()`, the owner's verdicts and the balance numbers in `TWEAKS.md` section G. All of those would need to be measured again. It also adds four bars to a screen that is meant to be quick to read.

## A cheaper version

Keep one crowd number, but give each model a "who is in the building" line and let two of the four groups move the number a little. That gives most of the flavour and none of the re-balancing.
